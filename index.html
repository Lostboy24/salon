Our Story

Crafted with passion,
refined by expertise

Founded in 2009 by master stylist Isabelle Marchand, Lumière was born from a single belief: that every client deserves an experience as beautiful as the results. Nestled in the heart of Montclair, our salon has become a destination for those who understand the difference between a haircut and an artform.

We invest in ongoing education, the finest European techniques, and products that honour the integrity of your hair. From a first visit to your hundredth, Lumière remains a place where you are truly seen.
